Vice Nights: Life Sim
Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 26, 2026

Vice Nights: Life Sim ("Vice Nights", "the game") is a life-simulation game developed by Fortune Ewuru. This policy applies to the game's iOS and Android versions. It does not replace the policies of our other apps.

Information in the game

The game does not require an account. Your character, settings, careers, save slots and cash balances are stored locally on your device. We do not run a server that receives or stores your saved games. If you choose a photo (for example as a profile picture), it is used on your device and is not uploaded to us. In-game conversations, social feeds and relationships are simulated gameplay, not messages to other people.

Advertising

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob, which may use mediation partners (AppLovin, Meta Audience Network, Unity Ads and Mintegral) to fill ad requests. On iOS the game asks for your permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt before any ad SDK starts. If you allow tracking, these services may use your device's advertising identifier, coarse device information, ad interactions and diagnostics to show more relevant ads, measure ad performance, limit ad frequency and prevent fraud. If you decline, only non-personalized ads are requested. Where required (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland), a consent form from Google lets you choose how your data is used for ads. Premium and God Mode remove the full-screen ads between weeks. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services. You can reset or limit your advertising identifier in your device settings at any time.

Purchases

Optional purchases are processed by Apple or Google. RevenueCat validates purchase receipts and manages access to Premium and God Mode. It processes purchase history, product and transaction identifiers, purchase and expiry dates, an app-generated anonymous customer identifier, and technical information such as app version, platform, locale and currency. We use these records to deliver and restore purchases and to prevent duplicate cash grants. Payment card details are handled by your app store and are never received by us. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

What we do not do

We do not sell your personal information. We do not ask for your name, email address, location, contacts or birth date inside the game, and we do not use cloud crash reporting.

Support requests

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to send, and use it only to answer your request. We keep that correspondence only as long as needed for that purpose or legal obligations.

Your choices and deletion

There is no account to delete: all game data lives on your device. You can delete it at any time by deleting the app or clearing its data in your device settings. To request deletion of purchase-related records or support emails, write to us with the app name and your purchase order reference (never send passwords or card details). Some transaction records may need to be kept for legal reasons. Deleting data does not cancel a subscription; manage subscriptions in your App Store or Google Play account.

Age

The game is rated for adults and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

We will update this page if the game's data practices change. The effective date above shows the latest version.
