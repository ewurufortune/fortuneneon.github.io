Essential Popstar 2.5
Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 7, 2026

Essential Popstar 2.5 is a life-simulation game developed by Fortune Ewuru. This policy applies to Essential Popstar 2.5, including its Android and iOS versions. It does not replace the policies of our other apps.

Information in the game

The current version does not require an account. Your character, settings, relationships and career progress are stored locally on your device. We do not operate a service that receives or stores those saved games. In-game conversations, social posts and relationships are simulated gameplay, not messages sent to other users or social networks.

Collection, advertising and tracking

The current version does not send personal information to the developer, include advertising services, or use developer-operated analytics or cloud crash reporting. We do not sell or share personal information for advertising, and do not track you across apps or websites.

Your device and app store

Apple, Google and your device provider may process download information, backups or diagnostics under their own settings and privacy policies. These platform services are separate from the game's local save system. Device backup settings may affect whether game data is restored after reinstallation.

Support requests

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send. We use it to answer your request and resolve the issue. We retain support correspondence only as needed for that purpose or applicable legal obligations. Please avoid sending passwords or unnecessary sensitive information.

Your choices

You can remove local game data using your device's app-data controls or by deleting the app where that removes its data. Manage any device or cloud backups separately. You may request deletion of support correspondence by contacting us.

Children's privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the game. If a child has sent personal information in a support request, a parent or guardian can contact us to request its deletion.

Changes

We will update this policy if the game's data practices change. The effective date above identifies the latest version.
