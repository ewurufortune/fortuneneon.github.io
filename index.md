## Privacy Policy for Essential Popstar 2

Updated November 1, 2026. Published by Fortune Neon, Nigeria.

### Your game data
The game does not require a player account. Character details, settings, game progress, cash balances and save slots are stored on your device. Images you select for cover art are used locally and are not uploaded to our servers. If you export a save, you choose its destination using your device's sharing controls.

### Purchases and subscriptions
We use RevenueCat and your app store to validate purchases, deliver purchased items and determine subscription access. RevenueCat processes an app-generated anonymous customer identifier, product and transaction identifiers, purchase dates, subscription status, and technical information needed to provide the purchase service, such as app and operating-system versions. We do not receive your payment-card details. See [RevenueCat's privacy policy](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy) and your app store's privacy policy for their practices.

There is no separate player-account backend or cloud save service. Restoring a subscription does not restore deleted careers or consumed game cash. Local purchase records help avoid duplicate delivery on the same installation.

### Advertising
The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob, including optional rewarded ads that give in-game cash. Premium members do not see ads between turns. To serve, measure and limit ads and prevent fraud, Google may process your device's advertising identifier, IP address, approximate location, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent through Google's consent message before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice later. On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings. The game does not ask iOS users for tracking permission, so ads on iOS are not personalised using your advertising identifier. See [how Google uses information from apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites) and [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

### Other services and support
This version does not use Sentry. Opening external websites is subject to those websites' policies. If you email support, we use your message and email address to respond and resolve your request.

### Your choices
You can remove local game data by clearing app storage or uninstalling the app. This does not cancel a subscription or delete records held by Apple, Google or RevenueCat. You can limit personalised ads in your device's ad settings. Manage subscriptions in your store account. Contact us about access or deletion requests for information processed through our purchase service; store transaction records may be retained for purchase validation or legal obligations.

### Younger players
The game is intended for players aged 13 and older. It does not require players to provide a birth date. Please contact us if you believe a child under 13 has sent personal information to us.

### Contact and updates
Email [ugonnaewuru@gmail.com](mailto:ugonnaewuru@gmail.com) for privacy questions or requests. We will update this page when our data practices change.
