## Privacy Policy for Essential Popstar 2

Essential Popstar 2 is published by Fortune Neon. The game does not require an account, and we do not collect or share personal information through the game. It does not use Sentry, advertising SDKs or third-party analytics.

Your character, settings and game progress are saved locally on your device. If you choose an image from your photo library for cover art, the selected image is used locally in the game and is not uploaded to our servers. You can remove local game data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it.

If you choose to export or share a game save, your device's sharing controls let you choose its destination. External websites opened from the game have their own privacy policies. If you contact us by email, we use the information you send to respond to your request.

For privacy questions, contact ugonnaewuru@gmail.com.
