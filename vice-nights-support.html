Support

Need help with the game, a purchase or a subscription? Email ugonnaewuru@gmail.com and include your device model and a short description. We usually reply within two business days.

Common questions

I bought Premium or God Mode on another device. Open the Store in the game and tap Restore purchases while signed in with the same Apple ID or Google account.

How do I cancel a subscription? On iPhone: Settings, your name, Subscriptions. On Android: Google Play, Payments and subscriptions. You keep your benefits until the end of the paid period.

My cash pack didn't arrive. Open the Store from inside the career you bought it for; any cash still waiting is added then.

How do I delete my data? The game has no account. All data is on your device: delete the app to remove it. See the privacy policy for purchase records.
